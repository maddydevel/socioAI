## Getting around

The app has four sections:

| Section | Icon | Use it to |
|---|---|---|
| **Today** | Newspaper | Read the briefing and the feed of updates by category |
| **Accounts** | Two people | Browse all 86 accounts and subreddits, open their profiles, tick follows, star favourites |
| **Follow** | Check circle | Work through follows one account at a time, per platform |
| **You** | Person | See your progress, connect your accounts, set up browser capture, change the theme |

Where the sections appear depends on your screen size:

| Screen | Layout |
|---|---|
| Phone (under 768px) | Sections in a bar at the bottom. Categories as a swipeable row of chips, and a **Filters** button. |
| Tablet (768px and up) | Sections in the top bar |
| Laptop (1024px and up) | A **filter sidebar** on the left |
| Wide screen (1280px and up) | Also a **progress panel** on the right: follow progress, your accounts, starred accounts |

Each section has its own address (`#today`, `#accounts`, `#follow`, `#you`). The browser's Back button works, and you can bookmark or share a section.

## Today

### Your AI briefing (the top card)

- **Date:** the day of the latest data.
- **Summary:** how many updates and accounts are in the feed. With the extension, it also says how many posts you saved from social sites.
- **Start briefing:** opens the story-by-story reader. If you stopped partway, it says **Resume at story N**, with **Start over** next to it.
- **Category bar:** the coloured bar shows how the updates split across the six categories. The numbers are listed underneath it.
- **Your feeds:** once you've connected or linked accounts, there are buttons for your own X home, X notifications, LinkedIn feed, Reddit home and Instagram feed.
- If the extension isn't installed, a line invites you to **Add the browser extension**.

### The feed

Updates are grouped into six categories, newest first:

- US & Europe AI companies
- Chinese AI companies
- CEOs & industry leaders
- AI researchers & builders
- Emerging AI labs
- Reddit communities

| Part of a category | What it does |
|---|---|
| Category heading and count | How many updates match your filters |
| **Live** with the X icon | Opens one X search of every account in that category, newest first, in a new tab |
| **Live** with the Reddit icon | Opens that category's subreddits combined, newest first (shown where the category has subreddits) |
| **Show N more from…** | Expands the category from 4 updates to all of them. Press again to show fewer. |

Each update card shows:

| Part | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Avatar and **name** | The account. Press the name to open [Account details](#account-details). |
| Source label | **Official blog** (the account's own site), **News** (coverage by others), **Reddit**, or, with the extension, **Post on X**, **LinkedIn post** or **Instagram post** |
| "via …" and time | Where it came from and how long ago. Posts saved by the extension without an exact time say "seen 2h ago". |
| Headline | Opens the original in a new tab |
| Name tags | Other listed accounts mentioned in a Reddit post. Press one to open its details. |
| **Follow** with the X icon | Opens the account on X so you can follow it. Shown until you mark it followed on X. |
| Star | Stars the account (see [Stars](#stars)) |

## The briefing

The briefing is a full-screen reader with up to 18 stories: the newest three from each category, one per account. It ends with a **You're caught up** card.

| Control | Action |
|---|---|
| **Next** / **Previous**, or **→** / **←** | Move between stories |
| Swipe left or right | Move between stories on a touch screen |
| Progress bar at the top | One segment per story. Filled segments are done. |
| **Read the story** | Opens the original in a new tab |
| **Follow @handle** | Opens the account on X. Shows **Following @handle** once marked followed. |
| Star | Stars the account |
| ✕ or **Esc** | Closes the briefing. Your position is remembered. |
| **Back to Today** / **Start follow sprint** | Shown on the last card |

## Filters and search

On a laptop the filters are in the left sidebar. On a phone or tablet, use the category chips under the search box, and press **Filters** (the three-line icon) for the rest. A red number on that button shows how many filters are active.

| Filter | Options | Applies to |
|---|---|---|
| **Categories** | All, or one of the six | Today, Accounts, Follow |
| **Source** | All, **Social posts** (only with the extension), Official blogs, News, Reddit | Today |
| **Accounts on** | All platforms, On X, On LinkedIn, On Instagram, On Reddit | All sections (keeps only accounts with a profile there) |
| **Starred accounts only** | On / off | All sections |
| **Clear filters** | Appears when any filter is on | Resets everything |

**Search:** type in **Search accounts and headlines**, or press **/** to jump there. It matches account names, roles and handles, plus headlines and sources in Today.

**Showing one account:** **Show all in Today** (in account details) or **N recent updates** (in Accounts) filters Today to that account. A chip **Showing updates about [name] ✕** appears, and pressing it removes the filter.

## Accounts

There's one card for each account and subreddit:

| Part | What it does |
|---|---|
| Name | Opens [Account details](#account-details) |
| Star | Stars the account |
| Category | Which of the six categories it's in |
| **N recent updates** | Shows that account's updates in Today |
| Platform rows: X, LinkedIn, Instagram, Reddit | The left part (handle or "Page") opens the profile in a new tab. The right part (**+** or **✓**) marks it followed or not. "Not on [platform]" means the account has no official profile there. |

### Account details

Press any account name to open its details. It opens as a sheet from the bottom on phones and a centred window on larger screens. It contains:

- its profiles on every platform, each with a follow tick;
- a star;
- its 5 most recent updates;
- **Show all in Today**.

Close it with ✕, **Esc**, a tap outside it, or the phone's back gesture.

### Stars

You can star **up to five** accounts: the ones you want notifications from. Starring shows a reminder to turn on that account's notifications on X; open their profile and press the bell. Starred accounts appear in the right panel on wide screens, and the **Starred accounts only** filter shows just them.

## Follow (the follow sprint)

The quickest way to follow everything, one account at a time.

1. Choose a platform: **X**, **LinkedIn**, **Instagram** or **Reddit**. Each button shows done / total, for example `12/75`.
2. The ring shows your progress on that platform. If you've connected or linked your account, there's a link to **Your Following list**, so you can check.
3. The current account is shown large:
   - **Open on X** opens its profile. Follow it there and come back.
   - **Mark followed** ticks it and moves to the next account.
   - **Skip** moves it to the end of the queue.
4. The queue on the right lists every account. Press the round button to tick or untick any of them directly.
5. When all are done, you'll see **All done on X**.

Category and other filters also apply, so you can do a sprint for just "China" or "Starred".

## You

### Follow progress

A ring shows all profile links followed, for example `40 of 165`, with one bar per platform and how many accounts are starred.

### Connected accounts (with the server) / Linked accounts (without it)

What you see for each platform depends on how you run the app:

| State | What you see | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| **Verified by X** (green shield) | Your name, handle, picture and when the platform confirmed it, plus links to your profile, who you follow and notifications | **Disconnect X** signs you out of it in this app |
| **Not connected**, server set up | **Continue with X** with three short steps | Press it. Sign in and approve in the small window. It closes, and you're verified. |
| "isn't set up on this server yet" | The platform isn't configured on the server | Ask whoever runs it, or save your username instead (unverified) |
| **Not verified: @name** | A username you typed, not confirmed by the platform | Without the server this is the most you can do. With it, sign in to replace it. |

**If sign-in doesn't finish:**

- **Cancelled:** "You cancelled X sign-in. Try again when you're ready."
- **Pop-ups blocked:** sign-in continues in the same tab and brings you back to **You**.
- **Still waiting:** while the window is open, the button shows **Waiting for X…**, with **Cancel** and **Show sign-in window**.

Instagram sign-in only works for professional (business or creator) accounts.

### Posts from your browser

This panel is for the browser extension:

- **Not installed:** step-by-step install instructions. If you use your own address, there's also a **Copy address** button.
- **Connected:** the number of saved posts per platform, when the last one was saved, and quick links that collect posts as you look at them:
  - one X search per category;
  - your LinkedIn feed;
  - your Instagram feed;
  - all 7 subreddits.

### Appearance

**Match system**, **Light** or **Dark**. The icon at the top right of the header opens the same choice.

## Feedback, undo and coming back

- **Messages with Undo:** marking a follow, starring, or unlinking shows a short message at the bottom with **Undo**. It stays while your pointer is over it.
- **"Did you follow…?":** after you open a profile from the app and come back to the tab, a prompt asks, for example, "Back from X. Did you follow OpenAI?". Press **Mark followed** (or **Enter**) or **Not yet** (or **Esc**).
- **Briefing position:** remembered, so you can resume where you left off.

## Keyboard shortcuts

| Key | Action |
|---|---|
| **/** | Jump to search |
| **→** / **←** | Next and previous story in the briefing |
| **Esc** | Close the briefing, a sheet, the theme menu or the "Did you follow?" prompt |
| **Tab** / **Shift+Tab** | Move between controls. Focus is always visible. |
| **Enter** / **Space** | Press the focused button |

## Accessibility

- Every control works with a keyboard and has a visible focus outline.
- Screen readers hear labels for icon buttons, follow and star states, filter counts and progress values. Changes such as "OpenAI marked followed on X" are announced.
- Moving to another section moves focus to its heading.
- If your device asks for **reduced motion**, sliding, staggered and growing animations are switched off. Simple fades remain.
- Text keeps a contrast of at least 4.5:1 in both light and dark themes. Tap targets are at least 40–44px.
