# Quick start (no technical skills needed)

**AI Follow Desk** is a free add-on for Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge. Once it's added, you get a daily AI briefing and an easy way to follow the 79 people and companies on *The Follow List*. You don't need an account or a server, and nothing needs setting up.

It takes about **3 minutes**.

---

## Step 1. Download it

Click this link to download the app:

**[Download AI Follow Desk](https://github.com/maddydevel/socioAI/releases/latest/download/AI-Follow-Desk.zip)**

A file called `AI-Follow-Desk.zip` appears in your **Downloads** folder.

## Step 2. Unzip it

- **Windows:** right-click the file, choose **Extract All…**, then **Extract**.
- **Mac:** double-click the file.

You now have a folder called **AI-Follow-Desk**.

> Move this folder somewhere you'll keep it, such as **Documents**. Don't delete it later: Chrome uses it to run the app.

## Step 3. Add it to Chrome

1. Open Chrome, click the address bar at the top, type **chrome://extensions** and press **Enter**. In Edge, type **edge://extensions**.
2. At the top right, turn on **Developer mode**.
3. Click **Load unpacked**. It appears at the top left once Developer mode is on.
4. Select the **AI-Follow-Desk** folder from Step 2 and click **Select** (or **Open**).

**AI Follow Desk opens in a new tab by itself.** You're done.

> **Keep it handy:** click the puzzle-piece icon at the top right of Chrome, then the pin next to **AI Follow Desk**. From then on, one click on the red icon opens the app.

## Using it: three daily habits

### 1. Read your briefing (5–10 minutes)

On **Today**, press **Start briefing**. The latest AI stories appear one at a time. Press **Next**, swipe, or use the arrow keys. Press **Read the story** for the full article.

The app updates its stories by itself twice a day. To update right away, press **Update now** under *Your AI briefing*.

### 2. Follow the accounts (once, about 30 minutes)

Open **Follow** and choose **X**, **LinkedIn**, **Instagram** or **Reddit**. For each account:

1. Press **Open on X**.
2. Click **Follow** on X.
3. Come back to the app and press **Mark followed**.

The ring shows how far you've got. **Skip** saves an account for later.

### 3. Browse as usual

Use X, LinkedIn, Instagram and Reddit in Chrome the way you normally do. When you see a post from an account on your list, the app saves it, and it appears in **Today** within seconds. To see only these posts, choose **Social posts** in the filters.

The quickest way to collect posts is the **You** page: under **Posts from your browser**, press any of the buttons, such as **US & Europe** or **China**. Each one opens the newest posts from those accounts on X.

## Settings (all optional)

Everything is on the **You** page:

| Setting | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Appearance** | Match system, Light or Dark |
| **Posts from your browser** | Turn saving off for any site, or delete all saved posts |
| **Linked accounts** | Type your own X, LinkedIn, Instagram or Reddit username. The app then shows shortcuts to your own feeds and following lists. |
| **Stars** (on any account) | Mark up to five favourites, then turn on their notifications on X |

---

## Your privacy

- **Your information stays on your computer.** Your follows, stars, settings and saved posts are stored in Chrome on your computer. Nothing is sent to us or anyone else.
- **It never asks for your passwords.**
- **It only reads what you open.** It never browses X, LinkedIn, Instagram or Reddit on its own. It looks only at pages you open, and keeps only posts from accounts on the list.
- To get news, the app reads public news and blog feeds, the same way any news reader app does.

## Getting a new version

1. Download the zip again (Step 1) and unzip it.
2. Copy the new files **into your existing AI-Follow-Desk folder**, replacing the old ones. Keep the folder in the same place, so you keep your follows and settings.
3. Open **chrome://extensions** and press the **reload** arrow on **AI Follow Desk**.

## Removing it

Open **chrome://extensions** and click **Remove** on **AI Follow Desk**. This also deletes everything it saved. Then you can delete the folder.

## Common questions

**Chrome shows a note about "developer mode extensions". Is that a problem?**
No. Chrome shows it for any add-on you installed yourself rather than from the Chrome Web Store. Close the note and keep using the app.

**I closed the tab. How do I open the app again?**
Click the red AI Follow Desk icon at the top right of Chrome. If you can't see it, click the puzzle-piece icon first.

**The stories haven't changed since yesterday.**
Press **Update now** under *Your AI briefing*. If you see "Couldn't update", check your internet connection and try again.

**No social posts are showing.**
Open X, LinkedIn, Instagram or Reddit in the same Chrome and look at a post from an account on the list. Also check that saving is turned on under **You → Posts from your browser**.

**Does it work on my phone?**
No. Chrome add-ons only work on computers (Windows, Mac, Linux, ChromeOS).
