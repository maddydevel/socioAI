Connect AI Follow Desk

The extension hands the posts you've collected to AI Follow Desk pages only. It already works with localhost and with a local copy in a folder named socioAI or ai-follow-desk. Add your own address if you host AI Follow Desk elsewhere.

Copy it from the You screen in AI Follow Desk, for example https://follow.example.com

Chrome asks you to allow access to that address. The extension only runs its small connector there.
